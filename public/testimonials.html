The primary cause of our problem was a faulty gutter design in which water drained into a flat area in our backyard, which then leaked seeped into our basement window wells and eventually into our basement. In addition to our gutter problem, there was also a year’s worth of leaves, shingle grit and branches which had accumulated in our gutters and had clogged the primary downspouts.

The guys from All Eastern Iowa Gutter came to our home, evaluated our situation and provided us with a design idea and quote to solve our problem Well, the rain has continued to pour this summer and we have not had one single water problem since.

In addition, we elected to also install OxMesh on our gutters as well. I was skeptical at first, but knowing I have a family and young kids who depend on me, the last thing I want to do is risk potential injury while working from a ladder to clean out my gutters. Thanks to OxMesh our gutters have remained clean and water flow has been completely unobstructed.

Thanks All Eastern Iowa Gutter for the solution, and thanks for the peace of mind!
